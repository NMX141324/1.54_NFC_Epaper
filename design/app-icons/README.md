# APP 图标方案

使用内置 image_gen 生成。A：折纸 N；B：六色风景；C：深绿折纸画框。

用户已选择 A（折纸 N），已应用到 Android Launcher 的普通与自适应图标。`option-a.png` 为采用的原图；B、C 保留供查看。

## A

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE polished Android app icon concept for a Chinese NFC e-paper photo app called 纸间 NFC. Square 1024x1024 canvas, full-bleed warm ivory background with a softly rounded forest-jade green square emblem centered with generous adaptive-icon safe margins. The emblem is a tactile folded sheet of paper subtly forming an N, with three clean NFC radio arcs integrated at the upper right. Minimal Scandinavian editorial design, warm paper material and very restrained soft relief. Strong readable silhouette at 48 pixels, crisp contours, muted jade and ivory, no text, no letters, no gradients with glare, no phone mockup, no multiple icons, no watermarks. The result is a finished icon only, centered and beautifully balanced.

## B

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished Android app icon concept for an NFC six-color e-paper photography app. Square 1024x1024 canvas. Warm off-white rounded-square app tile with a simple thick jade green outline of a small square e-paper photograph; inside it an abstract landscape made from six confident flat ink shapes: near-black, ivory white, golden yellow, muted red, cobalt blue, leafy green. A tiny elegant NFC wave is seamlessly cut into one corner of the photograph frame. Quiet playful sophistication, refined flat graphic design with subtle paper texture, generous safe margins, excellent recognizability at 48 pixels. No words or letters, no extra objects, no phone mockup, no board, no multiple icons, no watermark. One icon fills the image.

## C

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE premium Android app icon concept for a compact NFC e-paper picture app. Square 1024x1024 image, full-bleed deep jade rounded-square tile. Center a sculptural cream paper ribbon folded into an abstract open picture frame, with a small golden-yellow paper corner and a tiny red accent. The fold and three curved negative-space cuts suggest wireless transfer flowing into paper. Elegant geometric minimalism, tactile paper edges, soft restrained depth, no glossy glass, no neon. Very few shapes, distinctive silhouette, centered with safe margins for adaptive icon masking, readable at 48 pixels. No text, no letters, no background scene, no phone mockup, no multiple icons, no watermark.
